## Proposed replacement conditions — not yet executed or accepted

The 24-hour observation and seven-day recovery-package minimum below are new explicit amendment terms proposed for parent review. They are not elapsed evidence, automatic authorization, or a waiver of unresolved consumers. Until accepted for an exact per-path scope, they replace no existing gate. Neither this document's merge nor all eight caller migrations automatically establishes that acceptance. Acceptance of this amendment authorizes only the bounded caller-disable milestones explicitly reviewed per path. Full amended Task15 completion criteria require a separate review and decision; required retained URLs or unresolved consumers do not close Task15 or unlock REF047/048.

Unknown clients must either keep a tested compatibility route or block removal of that route; “no games” is not a waiver. No other path is disabled concurrently.
3. **Shortened observation:** propose 24 hours after the controlled caller cutover, with affirmative synthetic journeys at T0, at least four hours later, and at/after T+24h including a cold restart. The general 30-day pre-permanent-deletion condition remains outside this proposal and unchanged. No existing artifact/log retention setting is altered; if an expiring CI artifact cannot satisfy this duration, save a hash-verified local recovery package before acceptance.
5. **Stop/revert:** any lost/duplicate write, incorrect error/success state, changed credential/session behavior, missing asset/global/event, duplicate listener, changed capture order, stale-game UI/write, unexplained request, inaccessible modal/focus regression, unknown caller needing a disabled entry, incomplete evidence or untested rollback blocks the row.
