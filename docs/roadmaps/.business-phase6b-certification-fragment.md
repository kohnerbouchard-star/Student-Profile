### Architecture and gameplay decisions

- Materials are physically segregated into canonical WIP at queue time; they are not represented by a client-authored or Business-only quantity field.
- Manufacturing hold tables are immutable operation evidence linking existing canonical authorities. They do not replace Inventory holdings, labor reservations, or equipment reservations.
- Labor cost is frozen for later cost-basis allocation only. Payroll remains recurring and independent of utilization.
- A queued job is not Player-visible through a live start route until completion and recovery behavior are certified. This avoids cutting Players over to a lifecycle that cannot yet settle or safely unwind.
- Existing instant production remains a bounded compatibility route until the complete timed lifecycle is ready for explicit retirement.
