# Player-value gates — mandatory addition across the Living World stack

Status: **PLANNED** · Revision: **1.1.0-planning** · Date: 2026-09-20

## Applicability and precedence

Read this companion with `00-MASTER-ROADMAP.md` and the selected workstream. It adds a player-value gate to every existing and new milestone. It does not replace any security, economic, migration, accessibility or runtime gate. The original master remains the complete technical baseline; its seven-track/62-milestone counts and original integration/dependency summary are superseded by the current README, manifest, this addendum and `09-integration-certification.md`. All other technical requirements remain in force. The repository's root instructions and authoritative beta/release controller still govern actual implementation and release.

The current program is eight product tracks, 64 product milestones, four foundation milestones and two integration gates: **70 milestones**. DELIGHT is cross-cutting work during development, not decoration postponed until the end.

## Required player-value evidence card

For each implementation scope and handoff record: milestone ID; player situation; intended benefit; the interesting choice or friction removed; visible payoff/callback; owning source of facts; failure/no-response/absence behavior; public/private fields; routine interactions added/removed; accessibility alternatives; observation method; evidence reference and limitation; unresolved experience defects.

Classify the work as player-facing or a technical enabler. A player-facing change must deliver at least one meaningful decision, emotional payoff, identity expression, understandable consequence, useful social interaction or reduction in friction. A technical enabler names its downstream player-facing milestone and cannot count alone as an enjoyment improvement. It still proceeds when necessary for correctness. Do not add fireworks or arbitrary rewards to justify infrastructure.

Before implementation, approve the hypothesis and test. Before completion, record actual evidence, including negative results. Unit tests prove logic; connected tests prove journeys; simulations probe coverage; human observation supports enjoyment claims. These are not substitutes for one another.

## Emotional journey and recurring examples

Arrival should offer curiosity and a viable next step. First income should make effort and options visible. A setback should be understandable and recoverable. Midgame should provide identity and new choices, not more maintenance. Crisis should reveal relationships and previous decisions without making recovery impossible. An ending should acknowledge different paths, not only wealth.

Illustrative treatments are not promises of existing content: first posted pay opens a payslip and optional congratulations; a completed visit adds a passport stamp; a familiar character recalls verified help; a local festival introduces a real available opportunity; a fixed-rate agreement is contrasted with changed public conditions. Always check the fact owner, currency, source visibility and actual terms. A narrative claim cannot substitute for a receipt or an eligible offer.

The governing principle is **automate repetition; preserve decisions**. Autopay and saved preferences require explicit consent, clear caps, reviewable future actions and revocation. Do not automate an assessed explanation, investment judgment or irreversible agreement by default. Optional presentation can be skipped without skipping a required transaction review.

## PILOT-01 additions

Retain the original four-week, two-country economic journey and all failure/recovery tests. Add DELIGHT-01/02/03 as explicit prerequisites. Within the pilot, implement a bounded first-payday memory, a distinct local moment in each country, one source-backed NPC callback, one optional low-stakes micro-event and one measured anti-chore improvement. Use existing REL-04 and WORLD-03 functionality for the first slices; record downstream DELIGHT coverage without falsely closing its full milestones.

Include a player who ignores optional flavor and a returning player. Both retain basic opportunities and comprehensible obligations. Record memorable-event recall, a freely chosen next goal, callback understanding, confusion and required routine interactions. The first observation round forms a baseline, not a proof that all students will enjoy the game. Do not make the pilot wait for DELIGHT-08, the full collectible set or advanced asset classes.

## SYSTEM-01 additions

Retain every original technical prerequisite and add DELIGHT-08. Require evidence from all eight product tracks, a closed experience-defect ledger for blocking issues, accessible presentation, no coercive engagement mechanics, fair access to core livelihood and a working return flow. Keep implementation, merge, staging, production and human-playtest evidence distinct.

No consented human playtest means enjoyment remains unverified. A release owner may explicitly choose a limited technical experiment, but cannot relabel absent enjoyment evidence as completed DELIGHT-08 or SYSTEM-01. Do not bypass existing release authorization to obtain participants.

## Playtest protocol and proposed targets

Use a small volunteer pilot, appropriate permissions and fictional profiles. Assess novice, experienced, exploration-focused, optimization-focused and returning-player scenarios rather than collecting sensitive personal classifications. Let participants attempt goals before giving help. Record assistance and observable failure. Ask what was enjoyable, memorable, confusing, repetitive and worth doing next; asking whether they would return must permit an unpressured no.

Before a round, agree which tasks require independent completion, the acceptable intervention burden and the target for reduction of routine clicks. DELIGHT-07 proposes 30% fewer routine interactions on a matched flow, subject to prospective calibration. Also look for a remembered choice/consequence, a recognizable character/place and a freely selected next action. Report participant counts and anonymized exceptions rather than presenting a small cohort as a statistically representative result. No fixed approval percentage or session-duration target may replace judgment about coercion, accessibility or unsafe consequences.

Use at least one revised round before final enjoyment acceptance. Preserve negative feedback, fixes and remaining limitations. School assignments, attendance, prizes and grades must not depend on favorable answers. Store only anonymized summaries in repository evidence; private research records follow the applicable authorized retention policy.

## Stop conditions

Stop the affected rollout when flavor fabricates economic outcomes, exposes private state, creates unfunded benefits, encourages compulsive trading, punishes absence, overwhelms attention or hides material terms. Retain successful receipts and player rights while disabling future content through the owning domain. Reproduce and fix an experience-blocking defect before claiming completion. A pleasant presentation never waives the original authority, safety or accessibility gates.

## Scope package and handoff addition

Append a `Player value` section to every future milestone scope and handoff using the evidence card above. Record `not yet observed` when appropriate. Use the exact implementation SHA, test environment and content version. Identify the receiving domain for every economic or eligibility consequence. Make the next player-facing acceptance scenario explicit, including decline, failure, pause and return cases.
